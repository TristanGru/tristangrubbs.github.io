STS Thesis Prospectus

Designing Opportunity Ecosystems: Professional Student Organizations for First-Generation Students

This thesis explores the barriers that limit first-generation students' engagement with professional student organizations and how institutions of higher education can address them. Using Bronfenbrenner's bioecological systems theory and Putnam's lens of social capital, I analyze how universities can build opportunity ecosystems that create equity between first-generation and continuing-education students. The accompanying technical project is a unified CIO hub for UVA that lowers barriers to club discovery and participation.

AI Research Paper

ESG and Public Sentiment Analysis for Predicting Short-Term Stock Movement

This project explores using AI to predict next-day stock price movements from environmental, social, and governance (ESG) sentiment extracted from financial news. Using FinBERT for sentiment analysis alongside market data, I built Logistic Regression and XGBoost models. Results show limited predictability with high uncertainty, underscoring the challenge of short-term stock prediction and the difficulty in analyzing ESG sentiment in the market.

Software Engineering Paper

HoosHub: Enhancing Student Organization Through a Centralized CIO Web Application

A team project developing a centralized mobile application to unify club discovery, communication, and management for UVA's Contracted Independent Organizations. HoosHub allows students to browse and join clubs, RSVP to events, and engage with organizations through a single interface, while providing CIO leaders with tools to manage pages, post announcements, and handle membership. Built with React Native, NodeJS, Firebase, and AWS S3.

Creative Writing – In Progress

Destiny's Champion

A fantasy novel weaving together two storylines: Asher, a 17-year-old villager whose life is shattered when monstrous beasts destroy his home, leaving him guided by a mysterious figure with yellow eyes; and Lillian, a princess of the wealthy Kingdom of Adesh, thrust into grueling survival training in a sacred oasis. Set in a world where nine gods once ruled, their disappearance leaving behind fractured kingdoms and stories of civil wars between divine sisters.
